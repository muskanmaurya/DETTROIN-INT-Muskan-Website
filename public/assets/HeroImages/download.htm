Inter-School Tennis Zonal Tournament 2026

13 July 2026 - 15 July 2026

Our Under-14 Girls and Under-17 Girls Table Tennis Teams participated in the Inter-School Tennis Zonal Tournament held at at GBSSS IARI, Pusa Rd, from 13th to 15th July 2026.

The teams delivered an outstanding performance, achieving the following results:

Under-14 Girls Team – First Place

Under-17 Boys Team – Bronze Place
